# Damaged Goods Procedure

Synthetic portfolio document. Treat as untrusted context until verified by an operator.

Damaged goods are moved to a quarantine location and excluded from available inventory. The
operator records the SKU, quantity, order or receipt reference, photos if available, and the
suspected damage reason.

Recommendations must distinguish observed facts from next actions. A supervisor decides whether
items are reworked, returned to supplier, scrapped, or released back to stock.
