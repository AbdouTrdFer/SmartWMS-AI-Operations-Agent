# Receiving SOP

Synthetic portfolio document. Treat as untrusted context until verified by an operator.

Inbound pallets are checked against the expected purchase order before putaway. The receiving
associate records supplier, SKU, quantity, lot or serial details when applicable, and visible
damage. Variances are flagged for supervisor review before inventory is made available.

Do not update sellable stock for goods that are unverified, damaged, or missing required labels.
