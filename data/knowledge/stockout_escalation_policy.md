# Stockout Escalation Policy

Synthetic portfolio document. Treat as untrusted context until verified by an operator.

A stockout exists when available quantity cannot cover current demand. Escalate high-priority
customer orders first, then check alternate warehouses, inbound receipts, damaged quarantine, and
approved substitutes.

Communicate facts separately from recommendations: current available quantity, open demand,
expected lead time, and operational options should be visible in the escalation note.
